# Licence review
phyloSMC's own sources, tests, examples and documentation are offered under MIT with Leonardo de Oliveira Martins's authorisation. The SMC loops, proposal policies, particle management and process transport were written for this project using published algorithms. No third-party GPL source implementation was identified in these files during the review.
The linked Curupixa dependency is also offered under MIT for its own contributions, retaining its BSD, MIT, Apache, CC0 and public-domain component notices. Its [MIT transition record](https://github.com/leomrtns/curupixa/blob/main/docs/mit-transition.md) documents the replacements and removals; in a recursive clone, read `external/curupixa/docs/mit-transition.md`. The distributed submodule pin must include that transition and the root-split/GTR interfaces used here.
Curupixa supplies the reused numerical, phylogenetic and sampling implementations. Paper references in the tutorial identify methods; they do not claim that source from the papers' accompanying applications was copied or relicensed. Historical GPL releases remain available under their original licences.
This is a source/dependency provenance review, not an exhaustive comparison against every external codebase or independent legal clearance. Preserve the top-level licence and the dependency's incorporated third-party notices when redistributing the software.
