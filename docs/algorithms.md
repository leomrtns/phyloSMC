# Algorithm definitions
The [mathematical tutorial](tutorial.md) is the authoritative description of the probability measures, normalising constants and proposal corrections. It covers unrooted and rooted topology priors, fixed root splits, joint parameter inference, clocks, annealed SMC and online taxon addition.
`src/phylosmc.c` sequences temperatures and online updates. `particle.c` constructs and evaluates states through Curupixa. `parameters.c` supplies hyperprior choices and parameter moves. `moves.c` handles rejuvenation and resampling, while `workers.c` distributes particle calculations. `observations.c` checks online correspondence and constructs subsets. `rooting.c` connects observation names to supplied or estimated root splits.
Curupixa supplies tree-prior sampling and probabilities, including the direct root-split conditional sampler. It also supplies tree operations, substitution models, clocks and numerical particle-weight kernels. Reusable mathematical calculations remain there; phyloSMC determines the inference policy.
